Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

TikTok API Test is a development and testing application used to test integration with TikTok APIs and TikTok Login Kit.

Information We Access

With your authorization, this application may access basic TikTok account information provided through the TikTok API, such as your display name, profile information, and other data permitted by the scopes you authorize.

How Information Is Used

Information accessed through TikTok is used only for API development, testing, and integration purposes.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising purposes.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect authentication tokens and information accessed through the TikTok API.

Revoking Access

Users may revoke this application's access to their TikTok account through their TikTok account settings.

Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the application developer.
